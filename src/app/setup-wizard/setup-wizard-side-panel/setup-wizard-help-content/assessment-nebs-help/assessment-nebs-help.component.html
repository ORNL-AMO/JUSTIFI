If operational benefits are found that cannot be associated with a specific energy efficiency measure, then they should be added here.

This could be used for both standalone Benefits found in facility walkthroughs and Benefits requiring the implementation of multiple energy efficiency measures. e.g. Implementing several measures results in being able to turn off equipment or meet a secondary goal.

Operational benefits can be added to the assessment with the " Add Benefit" button.

Clicking this button will give you two options for adding a new benefit:

  1. " Add Custom Benefit" will add a free‑form operational benefit to the assessment where the user can name the benefit and enter a direct financial impact in currency per year.
  2. " Search Multiple Benefits Database" will open a modal with a list of all the operational benefits that are currently in the tool.

Once a Benefit is entered, "Key Performance Metric Impacts" can be added. These are impacts that the Benefit has on performance metrics the company is tracking.

The " Search Key Performance Metrics" button will open a modal with a list of all performance metrics in the tool. If a metric is known to be typically associated with the Benefit, it will be identified with an .

Details about the performance metric and the impact the associated Benefit will have can then be entered on the screen.

A list of Benefits found to be associated with specific energy efficiency measures is provided at the bottom of the screen.
