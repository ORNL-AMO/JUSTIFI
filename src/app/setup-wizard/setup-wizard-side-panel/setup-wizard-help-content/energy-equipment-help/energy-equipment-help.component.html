Add equipment to the "Industrial System Inventory" for the selected facility.

This inventory focuses on equipment related to assessments that are being conducted: larger motor driven systems (pumps, fans, compressors), process heating equipment, steam equipment. Systems like lighting equipment, HVAC systems, and mobile equipment can also be tracked.

Additionally, this system likely supplies other systems or processes with their needs.

Use the "" buttons to connect this system to other equipment, systems and people within the organization.

Setting the "Industrial System" sets the energy-related fields in the system details form.

Utility Type sets the units available for Total Size.

Enter nameplate data for Total Size to begin estimating the energy used by the system.

Consumption Rate refers to the amount of compressed air used over one hour.

Consumption Rate refers to the amount of fuels consumed over one hour.

Consumption Rate refers to the amount of steam used over one hour.

Specific Power the approximate energy required to produce a given amount of compressed air. This depends on your compressor type and controls. Can be estimated in MEASUR.

Enthalpy the combination of the steam's internal energy and the work needed to make room for the material, compared to the standard state, in terms of mass. Can be estimated in MEASUR.

Higher Heating Value refers to the amount of energy released when a fuel is combusted. This can be estimated in MEASUR.

Operating Hours refers to the hours the equipment (not the entire facility) is operating.

Load Factor refers to the fraction of the nameplate size power that is being used.

Efficiency refers to the energy efficiency of the equipment.

Number of Equipment can be utilized if multiple equipment is about the same size.

Notes can be used to help start to take notes about the equipment and how it connects to end users and potential operational benefits.
