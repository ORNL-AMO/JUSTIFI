# JUSTIFI Domain Glossary

Use these terms consistently in code, docs, prompts, issues, and PRs.

## Core Terms

- JUSTIFI: the application for identifying, estimating, and reporting Multiple Benefits from manufacturing energy efficiency opportunities.
- Multiple Benefits (MBs): benefits beyond direct energy savings that may result from energy efficiency opportunities.
- Non-energy benefit (NEB): a specific non-energy benefit associated with an opportunity, assessment, facility, or company.
- KPI: key performance indicator. A high-level performance category important to a facility or organization.
- KPM: key performance metric. A measurable metric under a KPI.
- KPM impact: the effect a NEB or opportunity has on a KPM.

## Portfolio Terms

- User: local owner of saved JUSTIFI data.
- Company: top-level organization in a user portfolio.
- Facility: manufacturing site within a company.
- Contact: stakeholder associated with company, facility, assessment, equipment, KPI, or NEB work.
- Portfolio: dashboard area for managing saved companies, facilities, assessments, visits, and reports.

## Assessment Terms

- On-site visit: visit workflow that groups facility assessment work.
- Assessment: specific assessment within a visit or portfolio.
- Pre-visit: setup phase before on-site data collection.
- Data collection: phase where assessment, opportunity, and NEB data is entered.
- Data evaluation: phase where follow-up, reports, summaries, and custom report options are reviewed.
- Pre-assessment: preliminary assessment data collected before the on-site workflow.

## Equipment And Energy Terms

- Energy equipment: facility-level energy system or utility equipment.
- Process equipment: process or end-use equipment associated with facility operations.
- Energy opportunity: energy efficiency opportunity with utility savings, cost, payback, and related benefits.
- Utility energy use: tracked energy source, cost, savings, or related utility data.

## Reporting Terms

- Assessment report: report focused on one assessment.
- Visit report: report summarizing an on-site visit and its assessments.
- Executive summary: summary report focused on high-level findings and impacts.
- Custom report: user-configured report assembled from selected report sections.
- Backup file: JUSTIFI JSON export used for data backup or transfer.
- Template upload: Excel template import path using `JUSTIFI_project_template.xlsx`.

## Agent Notes

- Preserve established labels unless a product change explicitly renames a concept.
- Update this glossary when introducing new durable domain terms.
- Use "NEB" only when the surrounding context already makes "non-energy benefit" clear.
